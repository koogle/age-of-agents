Style constraint: preserve the original villager/primary-diorama soft Ghibli-leaning two-tone cel look. Use broad quiet flat colors, one restrained shadow plane, sparse fine brown ink and simplified painted forms. Color pop comes from hue/value separation and silhouette, never glossy realism, PBR, specular gradients, microtexture, dense fur, satin sheen or shiny bevels. Treat material readability as a simple painted cue, not a realistic material study.
