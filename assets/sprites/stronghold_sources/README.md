# Barbarian stronghold

The war bands' fortified headquarters on a later island: a timber great hall with
a steep thatched roof and a rust-red zigzag banner, a ring of sharpened stakes, a
gate under a bear pelt and two thatched watch towers. Drawn with FAL
`fal-ai/nano-banana-pro/edit` from the shipped town center and barracks as style
reference, cut out with `fal-ai/birefnet/v2`, and packed as row 1 of
`buildings_sanctuary.png` by `python3 scripts/pack_stronghold.py`. Prompts,
request IDs and rejected passes: `provenance.json`.
