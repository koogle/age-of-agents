# Decisions

Each entry records a design choice in one or two sentences. The current gameplay direction lives in [README.md](README.md#proposed-gameplay-loop).

Current choices are below; superseded designs are isolated at the end. [Rework lessons](docs/REWORK_LESSONS.md) trace the supporting PRs, direct user comments, and recommended checks; historical verification is not fresh verification of this checkout.

## Current decisions

- **First environmental danger (2026-10-05):** Jakob redirected the initial danger work from timed events to wolves and dangerous animals, then requested a bear. Implement territorial wolves and bears with explicit player hunting orders; the drought proposal is withdrawn. Subsequent direction sets one animal on the first island and more on the second: one starter wolf, then 2–4 animals (one bear and 1–3 wolves) per later island ([wildlife](docs/knowledge/wildlife.md)).

- **Feature preservation (2026-10-05):** Existing buildings, including Watchtower and Barracks, remain important to the game. Removing, hiding, disabling, or adding prerequisites to existing features requires Jakob’s explicit approval after a before/after impact review and before release.
- **Game direction:** Build a Greek strategy roguelike around island expansion, escalating threats, eventual defeat, and permanent progression. Fixed win-condition scenarios were an earlier proposal; survival runs are now the direction.
- **Player control:** Villagers and friendly units wait for orders; there are no LLM-controlled game entities. A gather order may continue onto nearby resources of the same kind.
- **Shared simulation:** Keep deterministic game rules in `crates/game`, shared by hosted, native, and browser-local games. Rendering never decides gameplay outcomes.
- **Rust client:** One wgpu client serves native and WebGL2 builds. The retired Three.js client and its asset pipeline are removed; `/` and `/play` both serve the Rust client.
- **Scope:** Prefer small, playable slices and direct typed data over a generic engine, ECS, recipe language, or broad technology matrix.
- **Spatial rules:** Buildings, live resources, units, and step targets claim exclusive cells; move destinations are reservations. Eight-neighbor routing cannot cut occupied corners.
- **Orders and costs:** Reject invalid commands atomically and reserve costs once. Stop or replacement orders keep cargo and construction progress.
- **Production:** Each building runs one task and holds up to five paid waiting tasks in submission order; cancelling a waiting task refunds its full cost. Unit production waits for a free adjacent cell, and both active and waiting trainees reserve housing.
- **Persistence:** Hosted games save authoritative snapshots in SQLite and respect `AGE_OF_AGENTS_DB`; native/browser-local simulations are currently in-memory. Backward compatibility is deferred until Jakob requests it: incompatible store versions reset, matching-version snapshots persist, and corrupt current saves remain errors ([#83](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/83), [#90](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/90)).
- **Current economy:** Direct gathering remains available alongside farm/mining-camp drop-offs and non-stacking local bonuses. Processing costs and quantities are initial balance values.
- **Fields:** Planting and replenishing fields require paid, explicit villager orders. Preparation workers continue into harvesting, and existing harvesters retain their assignment while paid replenishment is in progress; exhausted fields never replenish automatically and idle villagers never replant.
- **Starter economy:** A new game's first island supplies food, wood and stone, with timber and rations processing available. Expose every wood/stone/timber building initially, then expand the menu as exploration reveals the inputs for remaining construction materials ([PR #92](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/92), [economy guide](docs/knowledge/economy-and-queues.md)).
- **First departure:** A dock builds a four-passenger transport for 60 wood + 20 timber in 20 seconds, so departure does not require resources found only elsewhere. Count timber's raw wood inputs in reachable starter budgets ([#55](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/55), [#71](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/71)).
- **Transport and resources:** Ships carry four passengers plus 50 resources; island-local stores and stopped shore ship cargo fund that island’s jobs. Villagers keep personal loads until delivery, passengers retain identity and housing usage; historical island/ship inventory pooling remains removed ([#83](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/83)).
- **Passenger ownership:** A unit belongs to either the land-unit list or one ship manifest, never both; boarding reserves seats and departure cancels unfinished boarding. Landing computes valid shore cells before changing ownership and does not require a destination dock ([#71](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/71)).
- **Island progression:** Generate deterministic adjacent islands as ships approach the frontier, without a configured island-count cap; ships sail continuously and all settlements simulate in one coordinate space. Return shortcuts prefer the original dock, with cargo available locally while stopped at shore ([#90](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/90), [current map and limits](docs/CONTINUOUS_MAP.md)).
- **Globe overview:** Retain distant planet zoom and display discoveries cumulatively. Terrain, camera marker and click/touch navigation use the same invertible isometric projection and current map dimensions ([#89](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/89), [#90](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/90)).
- **Camera:** Use a fixed orthographic isometric angle with pan and zoom. Building art keeps its proportions within level footprint plots.
- **Art:** Aim for a painted Greek island diorama with soft cel shading, fine ink lines, and tilt-shift depth of field. Generated sprites and textures replace procedural placeholders.
- **Asset workflow:** Generate with FAL, then upscale and refine with ChatGPT’s image tool to remove diffusion artifacts while preserving the approved style. Models, provenance, and checks are documented in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md#asset-workflow).
- **Sprite quality:** Require at least 512×512 authored pixels for every action, facing, and construction frame. Upscaling and DPI metadata do not recover missing source detail.
- **Deployment:** Host on Modal with quality checks and production verification after merges to `master`.

- **Compact HUD:** Preserve painted medallions and parchment while aligning phone actions and globe/speed controls in one bottom band. Expand queues and submenus upward only when needed, and collapse construction choices during placement.

- **World scaling:** Retain layouts in memory for now, bound ground geometry to the camera and compress repeated snapshot terrain. Profile populated archipelagos before introducing streaming; starter-only benchmark numbers do not certify busy worlds ([map budgets](docs/CONTINUOUS_MAP.md)).
- **Reassignment and presentation:** A new gather/build/cultivate assignment unloads even a partial same-kind load first, retaining the intended task and waiting for a compatible drop site when necessary. Active gathering with partial cargo still uses the work animation; cargo alone does not identify the current task ([#47](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/47), [activity regression](docs/ACTIVITY_SPRITES_REVIEW.md)).
- **Placement safety:** Every new permanent footprint, including fields, must preserve existing walking and delivery routes before insertion/payment; reachability to the object alone is insufficient. Fogged building orders explore and revalidate the whole footprint before creating or paying for a foundation ([#46](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/46), [#79](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/79), [#80](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/80)).
- **UI state:** Distinguish action labels, active-task text, queued tasks and completed research, and explain disabled actions on touch as well as hover. Layout and hit regions share geometry; accessible DOM controls remain required but unimplemented ([#35](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/35), [#76](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/76), [#77](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/77), [#84](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/84)).
- **Integration:** Preserve overlapping accepted source behavior before regenerating the combined browser bundle; old branch output is not proof of the merged source. Keep implemented, tested, merged, deployed and independently verified states separate ([integration guide](docs/knowledge/build-integration-and-release.md)).
- **Project documentation (2026-10-05):** Maintain individual system guides, procedures, research and reusable learnings in `docs/knowledge/`, routed by its index and consulted before changes. Update the relevant guide throughout work, especially after developer steering, while keeping decisions concise and task status in the handoff ([knowledge index](docs/knowledge/INDEX.md)).

- Island inventories replace the shared resource pool; stopped shore ships contribute their 50-resource holds to local spending and accept villager deposits, with explicit dock-only transfers. Cargo stays aboard on arrival; training and research stay in buildings.
- **Selection modifiers:** Shift-drag replaces the unit selection with the enclosed units. Control-click/drag adds units on Windows/Linux, Command does so on Mac, and adding already selected units keeps them selected.
- **Movement presentation:** Local interpolation follows the simulation accumulator; remote playback retains unplayed samples, buffers two ticks after starvation, and catches up at at most 1.1× for short delays. Remote lag above eight ticks triggers an explicit resynchronization to the newest snapshot without animating the correction; a long outage cannot preserve both every historical frame and current responsiveness. Villagers use every gait pose and animate work at their authoritative interaction cell, with no visual movement toward the target.

## Superseded designs

These explain the path to the current design, not requirements to restore.

- **Cargo assignment v1 — superseded by #47:** #31 let partial same-kind cargo continue into a new assignment and allowed builders to carry goods without a drop site; unload-first behavior now covers both cases ([#31](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/31), [#47](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/47)).
- **Local cargo transport — superseded by #83:** #71 used a 200-good hold and explicit dock transfers; the initial expansion plan required founding supplies, local inventories and later trade. The unit-only/shared-pool design removed those requirements ([#71](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/71), [#83](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/83)).
- **Map exchange — superseded by #90:** #73 generated a destination on first transport completion and used immediate voyages with paused away islands; #83 shared resources but retained that travel model. Continuous frontier discovery, sailing and simultaneous simulation replaced both stages ([#73](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/73), [#90](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/90)).
- **Earlier client and world assumptions:** Canvas 2D/Three.js, free-position/smaller-grid worlds and fixed-scenario goals were earlier implementations/proposals. Current Rust architecture and roguelike direction take precedence; the user's [expanding-world instruction](https://github.com/koogle/age-of-agents/pull/49#discussion_r4174501951) explicitly rejected keeping the world small.

- **Save compatibility (2026-10-05):** Until Jakob requests backward compatibility, reset incompatible stores instead of maintaining migrations or historical deserialization defaults. Current-version saves persist across restarts; corrupt current state remains an error ([save guide](docs/knowledge/server-and-saves.md)).
