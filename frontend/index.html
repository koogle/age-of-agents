    Controls

    Tap a villager to select it; long-press or Shift-click adds to the selection, Shift-drag box-selects. Tap ground to move, a resource to gather, a foundation to help build. Drag pans, pinch or wheel zooms, Q/E or right-drag rotates. B builds, Esc cancels.
